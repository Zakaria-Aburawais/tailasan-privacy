Privacy Policy

Tailasan Studio — Stack Tower and City Lights
Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile games Stack Tower (com.zakura.stacktower) and City Lights (com.zakura.citylights) collect, why, and what choices you have. Both games are published by Tailasan Studio ("we", "us"). By installing and playing either game, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. We cannot identify you.

Information stored on your device

Both games save your progress locally on your phone so it survives closing the app. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us:

  • Stack Tower: high score, best level, coin balance, purchased themes, mission progress, sound preference, daily challenge results.
  • City Lights: levels completed, stars earned, hint balance, sound preference.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it permanently. There is no cloud backup and no way to restore it, so uninstalling means starting over.

Advertising

Both games are free and are funded by advertising through Google AdMob. Ads appear in two ways: short video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a reward (continuing a run in Stack Tower, or earning a hint in City Lights), and occasional full-screen ads between games in Stack Tower.

To serve these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, device type and operating system version, general location inferred from your IP address, basic app performance information such as launch time and stability, and how you interact with the app and its ads. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Your advertising choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads. You can reset the identifier or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required by law, both games show Google's consent form the first time you play, and your choice is respected on every subsequent session.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Declining to watch one never blocks progress — you can complete every level and every run without watching a single ad.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your data, email zak.aburawais@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We reply within 30 days.

Please tell us which game you played and, if you have it, your advertising ID. We do not need — and will not ask for — your name or any other personal detail.

What actually happens when you ask, and what you can do yourself straight away:

  • Your game progress is stored only on your own phone. Uninstalling the game erases it immediately and completely. We hold no copy and cannot erase it for you, because we never receive it.
  • Advertising data is held by Google, not by us. You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads, which severs the link between your device and previously collected ad data. You may also contact Google directly using the links in the Advertising section above.
  • If you email us, we will confirm in writing what we hold about you (which is nothing), and help you route any request to Google if you would like us to.

We want to be straightforward about this rather than imply we hold a database we can purge: there is no account to close and no record of you to delete on our side. The deletion controls that matter are on your device and with Google, and the steps above are how to use them.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Ad content is filtered through AdMob's content rating controls, and we exclude ad categories relating to gambling, alcohol and dating. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our games, please contact us and we will act on it.

Data we share or sell

We do not sell, rent or trade any data, because we do not hold any. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Security

Because game data stays on your own device and we operate no servers, there is no central database of player information that could be breached. Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
